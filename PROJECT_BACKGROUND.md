# Project Background

## Who I Am

Name: Ekeh Mercy
Role: Project Manager

## Who This Is For

This project is for me. It is a personal tool, not something built for a team or shared with clients.

## Why I Want This

My mornings start reactive. I open my laptop with tabs scattered across email, chat, and my task list, and I do not have one place to start from. I want a tool that keeps my work organized and pulls me out of reactive mode, so I can begin each day already knowing what matters.

## How I Want To Do It

A single landing page that I open every morning. One page, one glance, and I know the shape of my day.

## Style Preference

Clean and professional, with a color palette that fits a project manager working in tech. Cool, structured base tones paired with warm accent colors to keep the page approachable and easy to return to daily, not cold or overly corporate.

Recommended palette direction:
- Base: soft off-white or light grey background, deep slate or charcoal for text
- Structure: muted blue or teal for headers and section dividers
- Warmth: a warm accent such as terracotta, amber, or coral for highlights, buttons, or items needing attention
- Keep contrast high enough for a quick morning read, low enough to feel calm

## Text Preference

Recommended copy style for the page:
- Plain, direct sentences
- Written like a colleague handing me a summary, not a report
- No AI syntax or stock phrasing
- No em dashes
- No footnotes, citations, or reference material sections
